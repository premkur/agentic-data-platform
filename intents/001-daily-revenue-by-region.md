# Intent 001: Daily net revenue by region

## Who needs it and why
Finance wants to track daily performance by sales region and spot regional dips quickly.

## Desired outcome
A trusted, documented table of daily net revenue by region that analysts can query directly
or connect to a dashboard.

## Consumers and freshness expectations
Finance dashboard, refreshed daily. Data should be no more than 1 day old.

## Constraints
No customer PII. Must be tested. Must follow the revenue definition in the glossary.

## How we'll know it worked
Totals reconcile with captured minus refunded payments for non-cancelled orders.
